# Italian style guide (it-IT)

*For translators and reviewers of Aloessential in Italian. Applies to articles (`src/content/articles/it/`), interface text (`src/i18n/ui.ts`, `it`) and category text (`CATEGORY_I18N.it` in `src/data/categories.ts`). Version 1, 2026-10-22.*

## 1. Principle: adapt, do not transpose

A French sentence translated word for word is often wrong for an Italian reader, sometimes legally:

- **Regulation differs.** In Italy, supplements containing plants may carry the *effetti fisiologici* listed by the Ministero della Salute for botanicals, pending EU decisions. For aloe vera gel (*gel sine cute*) the ministerial list reads: *azione emolliente e lenitiva (sistema digerente), funzioni depurative dell'organismo, benessere della gola*. The French articles say aloe's own claims are "not authorised". In Italian, explain that these wordings are permitted by national rules **and** that they are not EFSA-evaluated proof of efficacy.
- **Vocabulary differs.** In everyday Italian *succo di aloe* is the drink made from gel; in regulatory Latin *succus* is the leaf exudate (latex). Say which one you mean.
- **Shopping context differs.** Aloe products are sold in *farmacia*, *parafarmacia* and *erboristeria* as well as supermarkets. Labels must be in Italian.
- **Climate differs.** Aloe can live outdoors all year on frost-free coasts and islands.

## 2. Register and tone

- **Address the reader with *tu*** (*scegli*, *rivolgiti*, *la tua pianta*), consistently in articles and interface. Same warmth as the French *vous*, which reads as distant if rendered with *Lei* on a lifestyle site.
- **Short sentences, concrete examples, no hype.** Never *miracoloso* except to deny it.
- **Health limits stay explicit:**
  - *non sostituisce il parere del medico*;
  - *rivolgiti a un medico o a un farmacista*.

## 3. Typography

| Rule | Italian | Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No space before `: ; ? !` | `Ecco cosa sapere:` | `Ecco cosa sapere :` (French spacing; the test suite rejects it) |
| Quotation marks | « caporali » for quotes and titles: `[«Come scegliere un gel di aloe vera»]` | French « spaced » quotes |
| Percent | `98%` (no space) | `98 %` |
| Decimal comma | `0,5 g` | `0.5 g` |
| Dates | `22 ottobre 2026` (generated by `Intl` in templates) | |
| Apostrophes | straight `'` in source files (as in the French files) | |
| Units | `g`, `mg`, `ml`, `°C` with a space after the number: `250 ml` | |

## 5. Links and sources

- **Internal links** point only to Italian articles (`/it/articoli/<slug>/`) that exist as files in `src/content/articles/it/`. Tests enforce this. If the target is not translated, rephrase without the link: never link an Italian reader to a French article from body text.
- **EU sources:** use the Italian EUR-Lex version, `https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/IT/TXT/?uri=CELEX:<number>`.
- **English-only sources** are labelled *(in inglese)*.

## 6. SEO text

- **Title:** natural Italian query wording, at most ~60 characters before " · Aloessential".
- **Description:** 50–160 characters (tested).
- **Slug:** Italian, lowercase, hyphenated, without articles where natural (`come-scegliere-gel-aloe-vera`). Chosen from Italian search intent (TRANSLATION_WORKFLOW.md §3), not from the French slug.
