# English style guide (en, UK audience)

*For translators and reviewers of Aloessential in English: articles (`src/content/articles/en/`), interface text (`src/i18n/ui.ts`, `en`) and category text (`CATEGORY_I18N.en`). Version 1, 2026-10-22.*

## 1. Audience and market

- **Readers:** the English version targets **readers in the UK**, which is where the LR shop link goes (`https://shop.lrworld.com/home/GB/en?ref=cros`, prices in GBP).
- **Locale settings:** `<html lang="en">` and hreflang `en` (any English reader), `og:locale` `en_GB`.
- **Spelling:** British throughout (*colour*, *favourable*, *moisturise*, *diarrhoea*, *decolourised*, *centre*, *fibre*).

## 2. Adapt, do not transpose: what differs in the UK

| Topic | French source says | UK version must say | Checked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EU Regulation 2021/468 (aloe-emodin, emodin, aloe leaf preparations with hydroxyanthracene derivatives) | applies | adopted after Brexit: **not in the GB version** of Annex III to Regulation 1925/2006 (legislation.gov.uk lists no aloe entry). Northern Ireland follows EU food law on this point | legislation.gov.uk, 2026-10-22; Northern Ireland statement to be confirmed by the reviewer |
| Health claims | EU register, Regulation 1924/2006 | **GB Nutrition and Health Claims Register**. No aloe-specific claim authorised; botanical claims "on hold" have a special status that is not evidence of efficacy (ASA guidance) | gov.uk, asa.org.uk |
| When to get help (sunburn, burns) | médecin, pharmacien | pharmacist, GP, **NHS 111**; urgent signs as listed on nhs.uk/conditions/sunburn | nhs.uk |
| Shops | pharmacie, parapharmacie | chemist, supermarket, health food shop | — |
| Growing aloe | can spend summers outside | **overwinters indoors** in the UK; summer outside only in a sheltered sunny spot | RHS |
| Labels | in French, EU address | ingredient list in English, UK or EU business address | — |

The Italian-specific *effetti fisiologici* do **not** apply in the UK.

## 3. Register and tone

- **Address the reader as *you*.** Friendly but not chatty, no exclamation marks, no hype; "miracle" only to deny it.
- **Health limits stay explicit:**
  - "does not replace advice from a doctor or pharmacist";
  - "speak to your GP".

## 4. Typography and conventions

| Rule | Example |
| --- | --- |
| No space before `: ; ? !` | `Here is what to know:` (tested for every translation) |
| Quotes | straight double quotes in source; Astro renders curly quotes |
| Linked article titles | plain link text, no quotes: `[How to choose an aloe vera gel](/en/articles/how-to-choose-aloe-vera-gel/)` |
| Percent | `98%` |
| Numbers | decimal point `0.5 g`; `250 ml` |
| Dates | `22 October 2026` (generated by templates, `en-GB`) |
| Headings | sentence case: `How to take it` |

## 6. Links, sources and SEO

- **Internal links** point only to English articles (`/en/articles/<slug>/`) that exist as files in `src/content/articles/en/` (tested).
- **Sources:** use UK sources where the rule differs (legislation.gov.uk, gov.uk, NHS, ASA, RHS); EUR-Lex in English for EU texts, labelled "(EU)".
- **Title:** what a UK reader would type, at most ~60 characters.
- **Description:** 50–160 characters.
- **Slug:** English, lowercase, hyphenated.
