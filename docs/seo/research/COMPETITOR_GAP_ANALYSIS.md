## Reading

- **AI Overviews:** all five SERPs open with one. Clicks will depend on offering what a summary cannot: an interactive result, a full table, sourced figures. That is the tools' design; it is not proof they will win clicks.
- **Health topics:** institutional sources (Ameli, DGE, hospitals) lead. For these clusters, Aloessential's credible edge is the tool and the sourcing, not authority. Expect slow progress.
- **Aloe watering:** this is the clearest gap. The results are user-generated or dated, and the topic carries no health risk.
