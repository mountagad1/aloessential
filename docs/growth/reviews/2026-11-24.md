### What changed on 2026-11-13

Daily impressions jumped from about 140 to 354, then reached 2,389 on 2026-11-20. Almost all of the increase is one query and one page:

| Query | Page | Impressions | Clicks | CTR | Position |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| healthy breakfast ideas | `/en/articles/healthy-breakfast-ideas/` | 7,208 | 10 | 0.14% | 6.4 |

- **Countries:** Czechia (1,249 impressions), Italy (725), Greece (692), the UK (500), Denmark (375), Cyprus, Estonia, Norway and others. The audience is English-language searchers across Europe, not only the UK.
- **Reading:** the page reached the first page of results for a very high-volume query. Its click-through rate is very low for position 6.
  - **Plausible causes, not verified:** an AI Overview and image or recipe blocks above the organic results; a title that does not stand out; a short article (about 440 words) facing recipe sites.
  - **Caution:** the page has held this position for only 9 days. Do not rewrite its title in reaction to a week of data (`EXPERIMENTATION_FRAMEWORK.md`).

Without this page: about 3,200 impressions and 25 clicks. That is about 113 impressions a day, close to 6 times the baseline average (19 a day).

## 4. EXP-001: contextual links (published 2026-10-27)

**Comparison.** French article URLs, impressions per day:
- **before:** 2026-10-15 to 10-26, 12 days;
- **after:** 2026-10-28 to 11-21, 25 days.

| Group | Pages | Before (per day) | After (per day) | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Test (received links) | aloe drinks, aloe skin, joints, anti-ageing, hair, vitamins, morning routine | 54 (4.5) | 137 (5.5) | +22% |
| Control (already linked) | choosing gel, growing guide, digestion, homemade gel, hydration, juice, breakfast, after-sun, sleep | 152 (12.7) | 389 (15.6) | +23% |

**Result:**
- **No difference between the groups.** Both rose by about 22%, in line with the whole site warming up.
- **The success criterion is not met.** It requires the test group to grow at least 20 points more than the control.
- **Too early to call failure.** The hypothesis allows 28 days *after recrawl*, and the samples are tiny (a few impressions per page per day).

**Confounders:**
- On the same day, three articles were refreshed (aloe drinks in the test group; growing guide and hydration in the control).
- The tools and footer links went live the same day.

**Decision:** keep observing. Re-measure on 2026-12-22 with the same pages and the same method. No causal claim is made.
