You do not need a two-hour routine to get off to a good start. A few regular habits are often enough to change how a morning feels.

## 1. Look for daylight

Daylight, especially in the morning, helps set your body clock. Open the curtains as soon as you wake up, have breakfast near a window or walk outside for a few minutes.

## 2. Drink a big glass of water

After a night without drinking, a glass of water when you wake up is a simple way to start well. A slice of lemon or a few mint leaves make it more pleasant.

## 3. Move, even a little

Five minutes of stretching, a few mobility exercises or a short walk wake your body up gently. What matters is regularity, not intensity.

## 4. A breakfast that keeps you going

Combine a source of fibre (wholemeal bread, oats), a source of protein (yoghurt, egg, cottage cheese) and a piece of fruit. This combination helps you stay full until lunch. Some ideas in [Healthy breakfast ideas](/en/articles/healthy-breakfast-ideas/).

## 5. Put off screens

Checking notifications as soon as you wake up often puts you on edge. Try giving yourself fifteen minutes without your phone: time for a coffee, a shower, a moment to yourself.

## Start small

Pick just one of these habits and practise it for a week. One well-rooted habit is worth more than five abandoned resolutions.
