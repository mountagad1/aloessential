Aloe vera is one of the most common plants in our bathroom cabinets. Its gel, taken from the heart of the leaf, is more than 98% water, along with polysaccharides, vitamins and minerals. Here is what you can reasonably expect from it.

## Light, pleasant moisture

Aloe vera gel leaves a cool, non-greasy film on the skin. The polysaccharides it contains hold water at the surface of the skin, which feels comfortable straight away. It suits combination and oily skin particularly well, as these often dislike rich textures.

For dry skin, it works better as a first layer, under a richer cream that limits moisture loss.

## A soothing step after the sun

This is its best-known use: applying cool gel to skin that has caught a little too much sun. The cooling feeling brings relief, and several studies suggest a favourable effect on the discomfort of mild sunburn.

> Aloe vera soothes, but it does not protect. It never replaces a suitable sunscreen, or shade during the hottest hours.

If the burn is widespread, blistered or comes with a high temperature, speak to a pharmacist, your GP or NHS 111.

## Minor irritation and shaving

After shaving or waxing, a thin layer of gel can calm temporary redness. Choose a gel without alcohol or fragrance, which could sting skin that is already sensitised.

## Good habits

- **Patch test first** on a small area, such as the inside of your elbow, for 24 hours: allergies do happen, especially in people allergic to garlic, onion or tulips, plants long grouped with aloe in the lily family (aloe now belongs to the Asphodelaceae).
- **Read the ingredient list**: aloe vera should ideally come first.
- **Keep the gel somewhere cool** once opened, and use it within the period shown.

To pick the right product, see our checklist in [How to choose an aloe vera gel](/en/articles/how-to-choose-aloe-vera-gel/).

The same gentle principles apply to your scalp: see [our natural hair care routine](/en/articles/natural-hair-care/).

## In short

Aloe vera is a simple way to add light moisture and soothe. It is not a miracle cure, but a gentle step to fit sensibly into a well-thought-out routine.
