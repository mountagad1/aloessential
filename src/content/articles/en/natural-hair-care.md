Healthy hair often owes more to gentle handling than to a long list of products.

## Wash without stripping

A mild shampoo, applied mainly to the scalp, is enough in most cases. The lather that runs down as you rinse cleans the lengths. There is no need to wash every day if your hair does not need it.

## Useful natural ingredients

- **Aloe vera gel**, applied to the lengths, hydrates without weighing hair down.
- **Coconut oil** limits protein loss when applied before shampooing.
- **Diluted cider vinegar** as a final rinse can add shine.

Always try a new ingredient on a small strand first.

## Dry gently

Squeeze your hair in a microfibre towel or an old T-shirt rather than rubbing. If you use a hairdryer, set it to a moderate temperature and keep it at a distance.

## What happens inside

The state of your hair also reflects your diet, sleep and stress. Heavy or sudden hair loss is worth seeing your GP about: it can have medical causes that are simple to identify.
