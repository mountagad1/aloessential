A balanced breakfast is nothing complicated. It rests on three things: fibre, protein and a piece of fruit.

## Overnight oats with fruit

Oats, milk or a plant-based drink, a handful of berries and a few chopped almonds. Made the night before, it waits patiently in the fridge.

## Wholemeal toast

Wholemeal or sourdough bread, an egg or some cottage cheese, a few slices of tomato and a drizzle of olive oil. A savoury option that keeps you full for a long time.

## The indulgent yoghurt

Plain yoghurt, a spoonful of seeds (chia, flax, pumpkin), a seasonal fruit cut into pieces and a touch of honey if you need it.

## The complete smoothie

A banana, a handful of spinach, a yoghurt and some oats, blended together. Handy when you are not hungry when you wake up. Choose it over juice: the fibre of the whole fruit is kept.

## The express version

No time? A handful of nuts and a piece of fruit to take with you are better than nothing, or than a pastry eaten on the run.

## What if you are not hungry?

Breakfast is not compulsory. If you are not hungry in the morning, listen to your body and plan a balanced snack for later.
