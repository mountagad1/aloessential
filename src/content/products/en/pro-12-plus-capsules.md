PRO 12 + is the bacteria preparation in the LR Lifetakt range. LR builds it around what it calls a "TRIPLEBIOTIC" complex: fibre (fructo-oligosaccharides), twelve bacterial strains, and tyndallised Lactobacillus acidophilus, meaning bacteria inactivated by heat.

## What we know, and what cannot be promised

LR's product sheet has one real strength: it lists each strain and its amount per capsule. That is far from true of every product of this kind, and it is what makes comparison possible.

On the other hand, **no health claim** is authorised to date for lactic acid bacteria or bifidobacteria in food supplements, in Great Britain or in the EU. The evidence submitted was not judged sufficient. So we do not repeat the official sheet's wording on "general wellbeing" or gut comfort. We present this product for what it is: a precise list of ingredients, with no promise of an effect.

LR also says its capsules are "double encapsulated" so that their contents reach the gut intact. That is the manufacturer's information, which we cannot check.

## How to fit it into your day

One capsule a day is enough, ideally in the evening, with a glass of water. The pack of 30 capsules lasts a month. No need for the fridge: a dry cupboard is fine.

Lactic acid bacteria are also found naturally in yoghurt, kefir and raw sauerkraut. A diet rich in fibre (vegetables, pulses, wholegrains) remains the foundation. We cover this in [Digestion, liver, detox: what supplements can really promise](/en/articles/digestion-liver-detox-supplements/).

## Compared with the other products in our selection

- **LR LIFETAKT Liver Support**, from the same range, is also taken once a day in the evening, but has nothing in common with it: it is a formula of micronutrients (choline, vitamin B6, molybdenum, manganese) and plant extracts.

## Who it is not right for

- **Soya or lactose allergy or intolerance**: the capsule may contain traces.
- **A weakened immune system** (immunosuppressant treatment, chemotherapy, serious illness): ask your doctor before taking live bacteria.
- **Pregnancy, breastfeeding, children**: LR's sheet gives no specific guidance; ask a health professional.
- **Persistent digestive problems**: they need a GP appointment, not self-medication.
