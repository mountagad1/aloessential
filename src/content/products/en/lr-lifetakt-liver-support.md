Liver Support is part of the LR Lifetakt range. It is a single capsule, taken in the evening, that brings together four micronutrients and three plant-based ingredients.

## What is in the capsule

From a regulatory point of view, the heart of the formula is the micronutrients, all present in a significant amount:

- **choline** (82.5 mg), which contributes to the maintenance of normal liver function;
- **vitamin B6** (50% of the NRV), which contributes to normal cysteine synthesis;
- **molybdenum** (100% of the NRV), which contributes to normal sulphur amino acid metabolism;
- **manganese** (15% of the NRV), which contributes to the protection of cells from oxidative stress.

Alongside them, the formula contains wormwood extract (19%), milk thistle extract (8%) and chlorella powder (8%). LR's product sheet credits them with many benefits: liver regeneration, digestion, detoxification. None of these claims is authorised to date, and we leave them aside.

## What to expect from it

To be clear: no supplement "cleanses" the liver. The words "detox" and "detoxification", which feature heavily on the official sheet, do not match any authorised claim. What Liver Support provides is a daily dose of nutrients involved in the normal functioning of the liver and metabolism.

The rest depends mainly on your lifestyle: drinking less alcohol, eating a varied diet, moving regularly. We cover this in our article [Digestion, liver, detox: what supplements can really promise](/en/articles/digestion-liver-detox-supplements/).

## Compared with the other products in our selection

- **PRO 12 + Capsules**, from the same range, is about something else: lactic acid bacteria and bifidobacteria, with fibre (fructo-oligosaccharides). Both products are taken once a day, in the evening.

## Who it is not right for

- **Pregnancy and breastfeeding**: the formula contains plant extracts, including wormwood. Ask your doctor.
- **Children and teenagers**: LR's sheet does not cover use by them; keep the product out of the reach of young children.
- **Current treatment, or liver disease**: speak to your doctor before taking it. A supplement does not replace medical care.
- **Allergy to plants of the daisy family** (wormwood, milk thistle): be careful.
- **If you are looking for a quick "detox" effect**: this product is not designed for that, and no such claim is authorised.
